Home
/
First impression for HNW investors, advisory clients and partner advisors. Communicates Cotswold Capital’s position as a boutique global investment bank in a calm, polished, Apple-minimalist layout. White background, restrained typography, rose-gold accent on the logo, single hairline blue detail. No 3D objects. No illustrated graphics. No carousel.
7 sections 1 of 1
01Hero Statement
Full-width section with the firm’s positioning statement in serif type on white. Single understated call to action (“Enquire”). Logo top-left, no background imagery, no rotating slider.
02Who We Are
Three-line introduction to the firm: independent, network-led, multi-jurisdictional. Sets the tone before users explore services. Anchored link into the About page.
03What We Do Snapshot
Four restrained service tiles: Corporate Finance, Mergers & Acquisitions, Capital Raising, Investor Introductions. Each tile links to its anchored section on the What We Do page.
04Featured Opportunities
Three curated live opportunities chosen by Mark, surfaced as quiet cards with sector, scale and a one-line proposition. Each card routes to its full opportunity detail page.Client editable · HubSpot
05Track Record Strip
A small set of credibility headline numbers (transactions advised, total deal value, years of experience). Plain typography, no charts.
06Global Presence
Three-column block presenting the United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates and United States offices. Each links to the corresponding office block on the Contact page.
07Closing Enquiry CTA
Final calm call to action with a single line of copy and the Enquire button. Routes to the contact form, captured into HubSpot.

Developer & Build Notes
Platform · HubSpot Content Hub (Starter)Registered by Mark Hardwicke on the dedicated Cotswold Capital email. NEXA to receive partner seat access. Content Hub Starter supports up to thirty landing pages, which comfortably covers Phase 1.
Theme base · Human MagicBuild sits on the Human Magic theme (NEXA-owned) restyled for Cotswold Capital. Logo replaced, palette swapped to white background with rose-gold accent and a hint of conservative blue. Structure carries over; visual language does not.
Brand · pending colour codesMark to share the exact rose-gold hue and the conservative blue hue codes. Until received, design draft uses a holding rose-gold and a deep navy as proxies. Hold final design pass until codes are confirmed.
Design directionApple-style minimalism. White backgrounds throughout. No dark mode sections. No 3D objects. No illustrated graphics. Preserve and refine the existing Cotswold Capital logo mark; do not redraw it.
Content · written by clientMark Hardwicke is writing all copy. NEXA to advise on phrasing for multi-jurisdiction travel (UK, UAE, US) and LLM-legible structure for future Phase 2 work on LinkedIn outreach and OpenClaw integration.
Forms · HubSpot routingAll enquiry forms route into HubSpot with intent tags (investor, client, opportunity submission, advisor, press). Mark’s dedicated Cotswold Capital email channel is the only correspondence channel from NEXA. No Human Magic email threads.
Multi-entity disclosureOne website represents three legal entities (UK, UAE, New York). Footer and Contact must disclose all three. Regulatory disclaimers vary by jurisdiction; legal counsel to confirm final wording before launch.
Phase boundaryPhase 1 is the website only. SEO, LLM citation optimisation, OpenClaw and LinkedIn outreach automation are Phase 2 and scoped separately. Maintenance is not currently in the contract; flag to Mark for Phase 2.
Editable in HubSpot by clientFeatured Opportunities (Home), Live Opportunities grid, individual Opportunity Detail pages, Transactions grid (Experience), Advisor Network roster (Team). Mark publishes and retires content directly.
