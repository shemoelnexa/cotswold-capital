About the firm

A boutique investment bank, independent by design.

Cotswold Capital exists to give principals, founders and family offices the senior counsel of a global firm with the discretion of a private office. Three legal entities, one institutional standard.

02 · The firm

We built Cotswold Capital to advise the way principals actually want to be advised — bilaterally, in writing, by the partner taking the meeting.

The firm is structured as a federation of senior practitioners — each accountable for their own engagements, each underwriting a single standard of care.

We do not list every advisor publicly. We list every transaction we have led.

03 · Independent by design

A network, not a nameplate.

The network model trades the overhead of a bulge bracket for the breadth of senior advisors who have already built their reputations. The client pays for the conversation, not for the bench beneath it.

Every partner has skin in the engagement. Every introduction is reciprocal.

04 · Three locations, one standard

05 · Operating principles

Four principles, held in writing.

01

Discretion

Bilateral introductions, never broadcast. The right of refusal protects every party in the conversation.

02

Credibility

A partner takes the meeting and stays in the engagement from origination to close.

03

Independence

No placement bias, no captive distribution. We earn through outcome-tied advisory fees.

04

Long-term

Relationships measured in cycles, not transactions. Most clients return.
