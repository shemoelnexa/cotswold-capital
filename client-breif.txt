Brand direction
Reference benchmark: Apple website. Minimalist, sterile, polished.
Mood: Conservative, trustworthy, institutional, quiet confidence. No flourishes.
What it must NOT look like: A creative agency. No layered hero imagery, no decorative graphics behind text, no gimmicky transitions.
Replaces: Existing black + gold identity (Mark feels black reads "suspicious"; white reads "honest").



Colour palette
Background: White (primary).
Logo / primary accent: Rose gold (replacing existing gold). Hue codes pending from Mark.
Secondary accent: A specific shade of blue (Mark to provide), used sparingly. Accents only, no large blocks of blue.
Body text / structural: Neutral greyscale, restrained.



Typography
Editorial, classical, restrained. Sans-serif preferred for body, with the option of a refined serif for headings to add institutional weight. Pitch two directions in your first pass.



Site map (7–10 pages max)

Contact / inquiry forms: Embedded across the site. Two intent paths to surface: (a) clients seeking advisory services, (b) investors interested in current opportunities.
About Us: Communicate the network model without naming every advisor. Show the institutional shape, not the headshots.
