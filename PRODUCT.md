# Cotswold Capital — PRODUCT

> Design source of truth. Concept III: "Cotswold, photographed." — Apple-iPhone-page pattern, all-sans, image-dominant, vertical stack.

## Register

**brand** — marketing site. Design IS the product on this surface.

## Users

A family-office director, CFO, or HNW principal opening this on a 16" MacBook in daylight. They decide in 8 seconds whether Cotswold Capital is a real institution worth a meeting.

## Concept

**Cotswold, photographed.** Restraint as the design. Confident architectural photography does the visual work. Type, motion, and layout get out of the way.

## Disallowed (lessons from prior iterations)

- ❌ Serif typography or italic emphasis (no Fraunces, no editorial flourish)
- ❌ Horizontally pinned sections, marquees, dark contrast bands
- ❌ 3D objects, decorative WebGL
- ❌ Cinematic alternating panels with massive figures
- ❌ Multi-statement sticky-scroll storytelling
- ❌ Designed "moments" that say *look at this animation*
- ❌ Any pattern a creative-agency portfolio would use

## Allowed / required

- ✅ One large confident photograph per section
- ✅ Vertical stack, calm centered compositions
- ✅ Single sans-serif voice (Inter family)
- ✅ Smooth scroll, image fade-ups, number counters, hover micro-interactions
- ✅ Rose gold *only* on the mark; institutional blue *only* at inline links and status pills
- ✅ Generous whitespace, large scale, refined hierarchy

## Anti-references

Active Theory / ResnLabs portfolios. JP Morgan / Goldman / Morgan Stanley template sites. Stripe / a16z fintech aesthetic. Anything with marquees, horizontal pin, italic display flourishes, or 3D hero objects.

## Reference benchmark

Apple's iPhone product page — for the *pattern* (vertical stack, big imagery, sans-serif, restrained motion). Aman / Rosewood — for the *mood* (hospitality-grade stillness, confident photography).
