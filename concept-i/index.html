03 — Process

How the office works

Four phases. Held to standard.

Each engagement follows the same disciplined arc — written, agreed, and revisited at every stage, with minimal visibility throughout.

01 Engage Private introduction. Scope of the engagement agreed in writing before any action is taken.
02 Underwrite Assessment, structuring, and counterparty mapping. Principal-led, with independent judgement.
03 Execute Bilateral approach, terms, and negotiation under a defined and controlled process.
04 Close Documentation, completion, and ongoing counsel held across the cycle.
