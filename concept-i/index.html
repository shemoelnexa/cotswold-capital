03 — Process

How we work

Four phases. Held to standard.

Every engagement follows the same disciplined arc — written, agreed, and revisited at each gate.

01 Engage Confidential introduction. Mandate scope agreed in writing before any market action.
02 Underwrite Diligence, structuring, and counterparty mapping. Partner-led, no junior bench.
03 Execute Bilateral outreach, terms, and negotiation under a defined process.
04 Close Documentation, post-close coordination, ongoing counsel across the cycle.
