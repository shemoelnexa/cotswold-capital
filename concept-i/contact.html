Cotswolds Capital operates across a limited number of selected engagements at any given time. Most originate through established relationships or trusted intermediary networks, where context already exists and the basis for engagement is understood by both parties before any formal conversation begins.

Enquiries

Where a matter is appropriate and the timing is right, the firm will engage. Initial enquiries are reviewed directly by the principals. Where engagement is considered appropriate, a response will follow in the normal course.

Making an enquiry

The firm responds to enquiries that describe a specific situation — not a general interest in advisory services. The more clearly the nature of the matter is set out, the more usefully the firm can assess whether it is one it is positioned to assist with. Discretion and confidentiality are fundamental to the firm's operating approach and extend to all correspondence received, whether or not engagement follows.
