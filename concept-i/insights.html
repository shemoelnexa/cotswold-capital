Insights

The firm publishes occasionally.

When it does, it is because the matter is worth setting down. These notes reflect observations from practice — on transactions, on capital environments, on the dynamics of negotiation and the conditions under which complex situations resolve. They are written for people who work in these environments, not for a general audience.

Nothing published here is designed to attract attention. It is offered to those already paying it.
