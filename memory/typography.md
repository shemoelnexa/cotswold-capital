## Ownership update
No client typography given. **We are selecting the typefaces ourselves** and proposing them to Mark. See [[brand-ownership]].

## Direction
Editorial. Classical. Restrained. Institutional weight.

## Two directions to pitch (first pass deliverable)
1. **All-sans** — refined sans-serif across headings and body
2. **Serif headline + sans body** — refined serif for headings to add institutional weight, sans-serif for body

## Constraints
- No display/novelty faces
- No condensed or expanded extremes
- Body must be sans-serif (under both directions)
- Hierarchy through size and weight, not color

**Why:** A refined serif in headings can lean private-bank/old-money credibility, but only if it doesn't fight the Apple-restraint benchmark. Pitching both lets Mark choose without us pre-committing.
**How to apply:** Specimen both directions on the same Home + Opportunities layout so the comparison is apples-to-apples. See [[brand-direction]] and [[designer-deliverables]].
