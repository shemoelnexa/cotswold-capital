## Site map (cap: 7–10 pages)
1. **Home**
2. **About Us / Who We Are**
3. **What We Do**
4. **Services**
5. **Opportunities** *(dynamic — must support frequent additions)*
6. **Contact**
7. *(Optional)* **Insights / News** — only if it earns its place

## Key page treatments

### Opportunities — the credibility engine
- Clean, factual layout per opportunity
- Required fields: **project name, scale, sector, status, contact CTA**
- Design must accommodate frequent updates
- Showcase pieces:
  - **$4B anaerobic waste-to-energy plant**
  - **~$1B cremation industry venture**

### Contact / inquiry forms
- Embedded across the site (not only on /contact)
- **Two intent paths** to surface clearly:
  - (a) Clients seeking advisory services
  - (b) Investors interested in current opportunities

### About Us
- Communicate the **network model** without naming every advisor
- **Show the institutional shape, not the headshots**

**Why:** Opportunities + Home define the project — they're the two pages that must land first. Two-intent contact routing matters because mixing advisory clients and investors into one form muddies lead handling.
**How to apply:** First wireframe pass should be **Home + Opportunities**. About Us copy/design must resist "meet the team" defaults. See [[client-overview]] and [[designer-deliverables]].
