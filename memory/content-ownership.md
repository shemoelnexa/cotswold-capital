## Hard rule
**Mark Hardwicke writes all copy himself.** 20 years in the industry — let him.

## We are NOT writing copy on this project.

## Our content responsibilities
- Review phrasing
- Flag jurisdictional safety issues (UK / US / UAE financial regulation language)
- Capture LLM/SEO positioning notes for **phase two** (do not act on them in phase one — see [[scope-timeline]])

**Why:** Mark is the subject-matter authority and has explicitly retained copy. Writing copy for him — even "drafts to help" — wastes effort and risks misrepresenting regulated services.
**How to apply:** In wireframes use placeholder copy only ("[client copy]"), or short structural labels. Never propose finished marketing copy. If we spot a phrasing risk, raise it as a review note, don't rewrite.
