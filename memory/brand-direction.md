## Benchmark
**Apple website.** Polished. Minimalist. Sterile. Ultra-credible.

## Mood
Conservative. Trustworthy. Institutional. **Quiet confidence.** No flourishes.

## What it MUST NOT look like
- A creative agency
- No layered hero imagery
- No "clever picture-behind-words" layouts
- No decorative graphics behind text
- No gimmicky transitions
- No flashy motion

## Identity shift from current site
- **Old:** Black background + gold logo
- **New:** White background + rose gold logo + subtle blue accent
- **Mark's reasoning:** black reads "suspicious"; white reads "honest"

**Why:** Target audience is serious investors who need to feel institutional credibility on first impression. Anything that reads as "designed for the sake of design" undermines trust.
**How to apply:** When in doubt, strip it back further. Whitespace, hierarchy, and typography do the work. Any flourish must justify itself against the "would Apple do this?" test. See [[color-palette]] and [[typography]].
