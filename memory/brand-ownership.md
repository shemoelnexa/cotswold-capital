## Decision (2026-05-22)
The brief provided **direction only, no assets**:
- No logo files
- No font names
- No hex/RGB values for rose gold or blue
- No brand guidelines document
- No reference images from Mark yet

**Decision:** We are creating the brand foundation ourselves — palette values, typography pairing, logo treatment direction — rather than waiting on Mark. Output will be proposed to Mark for approval.

## What this means concretely
- Define exact rose gold + blue hex/RGB/HSL values (a small range to choose from, then a recommendation)
- Select specific typefaces for the two pitch directions (all-sans, serif-headline + sans-body) — see [[typography]]
- Recommend a logo treatment direction (color + lockup) — the rose gold replacement of the existing gold logo
- Package as a lightweight brand kit Mark can sign off on

## Guardrails
- Stay inside the established direction: Apple-level restraint, conservative, institutional ([[brand-direction]])
- Palette stays: white + rose gold + sparing blue + neutral grey ([[color-palette]])
- Don't lock anything Mark hasn't seen — propose, not impose

**Why:** Waiting on Mark for hues/fonts/logo art blocks every downstream deliverable (moodboard contextualization, type specimens, wireframe styling). Owning the brand foundation is the only way to unblock work without violating "right over quick".
**How to apply:** Any new brand decision (palette value, type choice, logo direction) is provisional until Mark signs off. Capture rationale alongside choices so the pitch is defensible.
