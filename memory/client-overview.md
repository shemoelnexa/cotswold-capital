**Client:** Cotswold Capital
**Owner / Lead:** Mark Hardwicke (20 years industry experience)

## What they are
A UK-headquartered boutique investment bank. Expanding into New York and the UAE under one unified brand.

## Services
- Corporate finance advisory
- Mergers & acquisitions (M&A)
- Capital raising
- Introducing high-net-worth investors to live opportunities

## Structure (critical positioning point)
Not one person. A **network of senior independent advisors** operating under one credible banner. The site must feel like an **institution, not a person**.

## Audience
Serious investors and corporates. When they land on the page they should immediately think *"this is a proper firm."*

**Why:** This shapes every design and content decision — institutional credibility over personality.
**How to apply:** Avoid hero photos of Mark, avoid "founder bio" framings, avoid headshot grids of advisors. Show the institutional shape, not the people. See [[brand-direction]] and [[site-map]].
