| Role | Color | Notes |

| Secondary accent | Conservative blue | Used sparingly — accents only, no large blocks of blue |

## Constraints
- Blue is an **accent only** — never large blocks or panels of blue
- No saturated/bright colors anywhere
- Greyscale should feel neutral and conservative, not warm or cool

**Why:** Blue is a "conservative/trust" cue, not a brand color. Mark explicitly limited it to accents to keep the page from feeling corporate-bank generic.
**How to apply:** Use blue at strokes, links, or tiny indicators only. Rose gold carries brand identity (logo, key marks). Everything else is white + greyscale. See [[brand-direction]] and [[brand-ownership]].
